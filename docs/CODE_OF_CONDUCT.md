# Code of Conduct
## PreRecoveryPath South Africa

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** December 2025

---

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders of the PreRecoveryPath SA community pledge to make participation in our project a **safe, respectful, and harassment-free experience** for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or recovery status.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

**Special Commitment:** As a platform serving vulnerable populations affected by substance abuse, we commit to extra sensitivity, compassion, and respect for those in recovery and their loved ones.

---

## Our Standards

### Positive Behaviors

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

**Respect and Compassion:**
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing when we make mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community and those we serve

**Inclusivity:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting people's pronouns and self-identification
- Valuing diverse perspectives and lived experiences
- Making space for underrepresented voices
- Acknowledging cultural differences and adapting respectfully

**Recovery-Supportive:**
- Treating people in recovery with dignity and respect
- Avoiding stigmatizing language about substance abuse
- Respecting privacy and confidentiality
- Celebrating recovery milestones and progress
- Understanding that recovery is not linear

**Ethical Focus:**
- Prioritizing user wellbeing over metrics or features
- Raising ethical concerns when they arise
- Supporting the ethics-first mission
- Transparency in decision-making
- Accountability when things go wrong

### Unacceptable Behaviors

The following behaviors are considered harassment and are unacceptable:

**Harassment and Discrimination:**
- Violence, threats of violence, or violent language
- Discriminatory jokes, language, or slurs
- Posting or displaying sexually explicit or violent material
- Personal attacks or insults
- Public or private harassment
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks

**Privacy Violations:**
- Publishing others' private information (doxing)
- Sharing others' recovery status without explicit permission
- Inappropriate photography or recording
- Unwanted sexual attention or advances

**Exploitation:**
- Taking advantage of vulnerable individuals
- Predatory behavior toward people in recovery
- Manipulative or deceptive conduct
- Using the platform for commercial exploitation

**Substance-Related:**
- Promoting substance use or providing access to substances
- Glorifying or romanticizing substance abuse
- Shaming or stigmatizing people struggling with addiction
- Sharing triggering content without warnings

**Project-Specific:**
- Advocating for addictive design patterns
- Undermining ethics safeguards
- Prioritizing engagement over user wellbeing
- Ignoring cultural sensitivity guidelines
- Bypassing privacy protections

## Enforcement Responsibilities

### Community Leaders

Community leaders (maintainers, moderators, ethics board members) are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

### Ethics Board Oversight

The **Ethics Board** has ultimate authority over enforcement decisions in cases involving:
- Harm to vulnerable users
- Violation of ethical principles
- Systemic or serious violations
- Disputes about enforcement actions

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (GitHub repositories, forums, Slack/Discord, email lists, in-person events, social media), and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns, please report it as soon as possible:

**Primary Reporting Channels:**
- **Email:** conduct@prerecoverypath.org.za
- **Anonymous Form:** [To be created - secure, encrypted]
- **Ethics Board:** ethics@prerecoverypath.org.za (for serious violations)
- **Emergency:** If immediate danger, contact local authorities first

**What to Include:**
- Your contact information (optional for anonymous reports)
- Names of people involved
- Description of the incident
- Date, time, and location (if applicable)
- Any supporting documentation (screenshots, logs, witnesses)
- Whether you've reported to anyone else
- Any other relevant context

**Confidentiality:** All reports will be handled with discretion. Reporters' identities will be protected unless they explicitly consent to disclosure or it's necessary to protect others from harm.

### Enforcement Process

1. **Acknowledgment (24 hours):** Reporter receives confirmation that report was received
2. **Investigation (7 days):** Gather information, interview involved parties
3. **Decision (14 days from report):** Determine if violation occurred and appropriate consequence
4. **Notification:** Inform reporter and subject of decision
5. **Appeal (optional, 14 days):** Subject may appeal to Ethics Board
6. **Implementation:** Consequence enacted
7. **Follow-up:** Check in with reporter on resolution satisfaction

**Timeframes may be extended for complex cases or during holidays, with communication to involved parties.**

### Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these guidelines in determining consequences:

#### 1. Correction

**Community Impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Consequence:** A private, written warning providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

#### 2. Warning

**Community Impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period (typically 30 days). This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels like social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

#### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period (typically 3-6 months). No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

#### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact:** Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community. This includes contribution to code, participation in discussions, attendance at events, and use of the platform.

**Special Cases:**
- **Harm to Vulnerable Users:** Exploitation or harm to people in recovery may result in immediate permanent ban and potential legal action.
- **Ethics Violations:** Serious violation of ethics principles (e.g., deliberately introducing addictive patterns) may result in permanent ban and ethics board investigation.
- **Privacy Violations:** Doxing or sharing others' recovery information may result in permanent ban and legal action.

## Restorative Justice Approach

Where appropriate and safe, we prefer **restorative over punitive approaches**:

**Restorative Options:**
- Facilitated dialogue between parties
- Acknowledgment of harm and sincere apology
- Agreement on changed behavior
- Community service (e.g., documentation, translation)
- Educational requirements (e.g., cultural sensitivity training)

**When NOT Appropriate:**
- Serious harm or safety concerns
- Power imbalances (e.g., exploitation)
- Lack of sincere acknowledgment
- Repeated violations
- Victim requests punitive approach

---

## Cultural Sensitivity

Given South Africa's diverse cultures, we commit to:

**Cultural Awareness:**
- Recognizing that norms vary across cultures
- Not imposing one culture's standards on others
- Seeking understanding before judging
- Consulting cultural experts when appropriate

**Language:**
- Supporting all 11 official SA languages in conduct processes
- Translation provided for non-English speakers
- Plain language used (no jargon or legalese)
- Audio options for low literacy

**Process Adaptations:**
- Culturally appropriate conflict resolution (e.g., ubuntu restorative circles)
- Community elder involvement (with permission)
- Respect for traditional authority
- Flexibility in communication styles

## Protection of Vulnerable Individuals

**Extra Protections for:**
- People in recovery (all stages)
- Family members of those affected by substance abuse
- Youth and minors
- Elderly community members
- People with low digital literacy
- People with mental health conditions
- Economically marginalized individuals

**Protections Include:**
- Lower threshold for intervention
- Enhanced confidentiality
- Trauma-informed approaches
- Connection to support resources
- Flexibility in participation
- Accommodation of accessibility needs

**Zero Tolerance:**
- Exploitation of vulnerability
- Predatory behavior
- Shaming or stigmatizing
- Taking advantage of desperation

## Appeals Process

**Right to Appeal:**
Anyone subject to enforcement action may appeal to the **Ethics Board** within 14 days of notification.

**Appeal Process:**
1. Submit written appeal to ethics@prerecoverypath.org.za
2. Ethics Board reviews (within 30 days)
3. May request additional information or interviews
4. Decision is final (no further appeal)
5. Outcome communicated in writing

**Grounds for Appeal:**
- Factual error in investigation
- Procedural unfairness
- Disproportionate consequence
- New evidence available

## Anonymous Reporting

We recognize that not everyone feels safe making non-anonymous reports. Anonymous reporting is supported with limitations:

**Anonymous Reporting:**
- Use secure, encrypted form [To be created]
- No identifying information required
- Still receive general updates (no personal identification)

**Limitations:**
- Cannot follow up for clarification
- Harder to investigate
- Cannot notify reporter of outcome directly

**When to Report Non-Anonymously:**
- If you're comfortable doing so
- When details/context are important
- If you want to be informed of outcome
