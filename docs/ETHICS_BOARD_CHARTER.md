# Ethics Board Charter
## PreRecoveryPath South Africa

**Adopted:** December 2025  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

## 1. Purpose and Mission

The PreRecoveryPath SA Ethics Board exists to ensure the platform serves the best interests of people affected by substance abuse, prioritizing user autonomy, safety, and wellbeing over all other considerations including growth, engagement, and revenue.

**Mission Statement:**
> To safeguard vulnerable populations from technological harm while maximizing the platform's potential to support genuine recovery and wellbeing.

---

## 2. Core Responsibilities

### 2.1 Oversight Authority

The Ethics Board has **veto power** over:
- All new features and functionality
- Algorithm changes or updates
- Data collection practices
- Partnership agreements
- Marketing materials and messaging
- Research protocols
- Platform design changes

### 2.2 Required Reviews

**Pre-Launch Review (Mandatory):**
- New features cannot launch without board approval
- Algorithm changes require ethical impact assessment
- Data practice modifications need consent review

**Quarterly Audits:**
- Usage pattern analysis (detecting platform addiction)
- Outcome metrics review (real-world impact)
- Privacy compliance verification
- Cultural sensitivity assessment
- Vulnerability protection effectiveness

**Incident Response:**
- Investigate ethical violations within 72 hours
- Require corrective action
- Issue public statements when appropriate

### 2.3 Transparency Requirements

**Public Reporting:**
- Quarterly transparency reports
- Annual comprehensive ethics review
- Incident summaries (anonymized)
- Board meeting minutes (redacted for privacy)

**Community Engagement:**
- Bi-annual community forums
- Open submission process for concerns
- User feedback integration

### 3.2 Selection Process

**Nomination:**
- Open call for nominations (self and community)
- Community organizations invited to nominate
- Current board members can nominate

**Selection Criteria:**
- Demonstrated commitment to ethical principles
- Relevant expertise and experience
- Diversity of perspectives (geographic, cultural, demographic)
- No conflicts of interest

**Appointment:**
- Selection committee reviews nominations
- Community input period (30 days)
- Final appointments by founding team (initial board)
- Subsequent appointments by sitting board (2/3 majority)

**Term Limits:**
- 3-year terms
- Maximum 2 consecutive terms
- Staggered terms for continuity
- 1/3 of board rotates annually

### 3.3 Compensation

**Stipends:**
- All board members receive fair compensation for time
- Travel expenses covered
- Childcare/eldercare support provided for meetings
- No volunteer expectation (recognizes expertise value)

**Suggested Compensation:**
- R2,000 per meeting attended
- R5,000 per quarter for committee chair roles
- All expenses reimbursed

## 4. Governance Structure

### 4.1 Board Officers

**Chairperson:**
- Elected by board members
- 2-year term
- Facilitates meetings, represents board publicly
- Cannot be from technology/product team

**Vice-Chairperson:**
- Elected by board members
- Assists chairperson, assumes duties if absent
- Different expertise area than chairperson

**Secretary:**
- Records meeting minutes
- Manages documentation
- Coordinates communications

### 4.2 Standing Committees

**Ethics Review Committee:**
- Reviews new features and algorithm changes
- 5-7 members
- Rapid review capability (72-hour turnaround for urgent items)

**Audit Committee:**
- Conducts quarterly usage and outcome audits
- 5 members
- Reports findings to full board

**Incident Response Committee:**
- Investigates ethical concerns and violations
- 5 members
- 72-hour investigation timeline

**Community Engagement Committee:**
- Organizes community forums
- Processes community feedback
- 5-7 members

### 4.3 Decision-Making

**Quorum:**
- 2/3 of members (19 of 28) required for official votes

**Voting:**
- Standard decisions: Simple majority of present members
- Veto decisions: 2/3 majority of present members
- Constitutional changes: 3/4 majority of full board

**Veto Authority:**
- Any board member can raise ethical concern
- Triggers automatic review
- Board can veto by 2/3 vote
- Veto is final (cannot be overridden by company)

## 6. Ethical Decision-Making Framework

### 6.1 Evaluation Criteria

When reviewing features, algorithms, or practices, board evaluates:

**1. Autonomy Preservation:**
- Does this respect user control?
- Are exit options clear and accessible?
- Can users override or decline?

**2. Manipulation Prevention:**
- Are we using addictive design patterns?
- Is engagement prioritized over user wellbeing?
- Is reasoning transparent?

**3. Human Connection Priority:**
- Does this support or replace human connection?
- Are we pushing users toward human support?
- Is AI positioned appropriately (supplementary)?

**4. Data Ethics:**
- Is data collection minimized?
- Is consent granular and informed?
- Can users access/delete their data?

**5. Cultural Sensitivity:**
- Is this appropriate across all SA cultures?
- Have we consulted relevant communities?
- Are we imposing external values?

**6. Vulnerability Protection:**
- Are extra safeguards in place for vulnerable users?
- Could this harm rather than help?
- Are risks clearly communicated?

**7. Real-World Impact:**
- Does this improve actual outcomes?
- Could this create platform dependency?
- Are we measuring what matters?

### 6.2 Decision Framework

```
Question Raised
    ↓
Preliminary Review (Committee)
    ↓
Risk Assessment
    ↓
Community Consultation (if needed)
    ↓
Full Board Discussion
    ↓
Vote
    ↓
Decision Communicated
    ↓
Implementation Monitoring
    ↓
Follow-up Review
```

## 7. Accountability and Oversight

### 7.1 Board Accountability

**To Users:**
- Quarterly transparency reports published
- User feedback mechanisms
- Complaint investigation process

**To Community:**
- Bi-annual community forums
- Open meeting minutes (privacy-redacted)
- Annual public report

**To Regulators:**
- POPIA compliance reporting
- Cooperation with government inquiries
- Academic research transparency

### 7.2 Removing Board Members

**Grounds for Removal:**
- Consistent absence (missing 3+ meetings without cause)
- Conflict of interest violation
- Breach of confidentiality
- Ethical misconduct

**Process:**
- Concern raised to chairperson
- Investigation by independent committee
- Full board vote (3/4 majority required)
- Transparent explanation (privacy-appropriate)

### 7.3 Platform Accountability to Board

**Required Reporting:**
- Monthly usage statistics
- Quarterly outcome metrics
- All algorithm changes
- Any ethical incidents
- User complaints related to ethics

**Board Access:**
- Full access to platform data (anonymized)
- Access to user complaints
- Technical documentation
- Code review access (for ethics-related code)

**Failure to Comply:**
- Board can recommend suspension of operations
- Public statement of non-compliance
- Escalation to regulators if needed

## 8. Confidentiality and Conflicts of Interest

### 8.1 Confidentiality

**Protected Information:**
- Individual user data (always)
- Proprietary algorithms (strategic)
- Unreleased features (pre-launch)
- Confidential complaints

**Public Information:**
- Board decisions and reasoning
- Aggregate usage statistics
- Ethical principles and policies
- Incident summaries (anonymized)

**Breach Consequences:**
- Immediate removal from board
- Legal action if POPIA violation
- Public statement

### 8.2 Conflicts of Interest

**Required Disclosure:**
- Financial interests in competing platforms
- Employment relationships
- Family relationships with platform team
- Research funding from platform

**Recusal Requirements:**
- Must recuse from votes where conflict exists
- Can provide input but cannot vote
- Disclosed in meeting minutes

## 9. Amendment Process

### 9.1 Charter Amendments

**Who Can Propose:**
- Any board member
- Platform leadership (with justification)
- Community petition (100+ signatures)

**Process:**
- Proposal circulated 30 days before vote
- Community comment period (30 days)
- Board discussion
- Vote required: 3/4 of full board (21 of 28)
- Public announcement of changes

### 9.2 Emergency Amendments

**In crisis situations:**
- 2/3 board vote can enact temporary amendments
- Must be ratified by full process within 90 days
- Transparent reasoning required

## 11. Success Metrics for Ethics Board

The board itself is evaluated on:

**Process Metrics:**
- Meeting attendance rates
- Review turnaround times
- Community engagement levels
- Transparency report timeliness

**Outcome Metrics:**
- User trust scores
- Ethical incident rates (should decrease)

**Impact Metrics:**

- Vulnerable population protection effectiveness
